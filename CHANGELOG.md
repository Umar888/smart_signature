## 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* `SignatureField`: signature input with a formatting toolbar on top, following light and dark mode.
* Smart enhance: removes jitter, pen hooks and stray marks, refits strokes as smooth curves and straightens on export. On-device, pluggable via `SignatureEnhancer`.
* Ballpoint, fountain and marker inks with speed or stylus pressure based width.
* Word style colour palette with a custom colour picker, width presets and slider.
* Partial and whole stroke eraser, undo and redo.
* PNG, SVG and JSON export, cropped to the ink, with optional background and ink colour override.
* `SignaturePad` and `SignatureController` for custom UIs.
